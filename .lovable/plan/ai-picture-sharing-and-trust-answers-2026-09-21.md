# AI Picture Sharing and Trust Answers

## Changes
- Add a picture attachment control to both the public Skyline AI and signed-in member AI chats.
- Accept one common image file at a time, show its preview before sending, allow removal, and support a picture-only question.
- Render sent pictures inside the conversation and include them in the AI’s full conversation context.
- Persist member-chat picture messages with their existing thread history; keep public-chat pictures temporary and cleared on refresh.
- Add strict size/type validation in the browser and again on both chat endpoints.

## Scam or fraud questions
- Respond calmly and respectfully instead of dismissing the concern.
- Explain only verified Skyline Achievers facts, the guided official-member process, and safe steps the person can use to verify identity and information.
- Never claim something is “100% real,” fabricate proof, pressure someone to stay, silence doubts, or promise earnings/results.
- Keep the existing rule that fee, investment, payment, salary, and earnings questions are referred to the person’s senior or official member without quoting amounts.

## Validation
- Test picture preview, removal, picture-only sending, AI image understanding, and conversation rendering on phone and desktop.
- Test scam/fraud questions in Roman Urdu and English for factual, reassuring, non-coercive answers.
- Confirm member history remains compatible and the website builds cleanly.
