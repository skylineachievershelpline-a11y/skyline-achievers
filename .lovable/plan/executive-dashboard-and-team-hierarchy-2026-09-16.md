# Executive dashboard and team hierarchy

## Goal
Upgrade the signed-in member experience to the selected Skyline Executive direction while preserving all current training, upload, invite, and member-management behavior.

## Dashboard
- Recompose the top area into a polished member identity panel with professional avatar treatment, member ID, rank, join date, and last activity.
- Add an at-a-glance tracking area using existing data: accessible levels, available series/lectures, active series, and watched lecture progress.
- Surface Continue Watching and current-rank series for direct access, followed by the existing training levels and daily inspiration.
- Keep avatar upload and app installation working.

## Team
- Restyle team statistics into a compact performance overview.
- Add a connected hierarchy view with the signed-in member as the root and direct trainees as child nodes.
- Show each trainee's status, session completion, ID, phone, join date, and existing block/unblock/remove actions.
- Retain seat reservation and registration-link controls in a cleaner workspace layout.
- On phones, use a vertically connected tree with full-width nodes so the hierarchy remains clear without tiny text or clipped controls.

## Design system
- Apply the selected clean Skyline-blue executive palette through semantic tokens.
- Use light neutral workspace surfaces, restrained green/pink/amber tracking accents, compact borders, and subtle shadows.
- Keep motion restrained and preserve reduced-motion behavior.

## Validation
- Check desktop and phone layouts for overflow, overlap, hierarchy clarity, and readable controls.
- Verify the dashboard and team pages compile and preserve existing actions.
