# Premium 3D Skyline visual system

## Goal
Transform the full Skyline Achievers interface into a premium 3D corporate infographic system while preserving the existing logo, content, routes, permissions, uploads, forms, and training behavior.

## Brand system
- Keep the existing logo asset and its proportions exactly unchanged.
- Restrict the palette to midnight navy, near black, electric royal blue, bright blue, cyan, white, and metallic silver.
- Replace the light executive override and unrelated status colors with brand-consistent blue/cyan/silver states.
- Introduce reusable layered surfaces: recessed background, raised platform, elevated panel, inset content area, and small raised indicators.
- Use crisp silver edges, realistic directional shadows, restrained blue accent lighting, and minimal blur.

## Public experience
- Preserve the current full-image opening screen, but give its navigation, trust strip, buttons, and labels clearer physical depth.
- Recompose the introduction into a connected raised media platform with supporting nodes.
- Turn the four-step story into an asymmetric connected journey instead of a flat card grid.
- Give testimonials distinct elevated formats while keeping video, review submission, and admin-managed content working.

## Member and training experience
- Apply the same dark premium identity to the member shell, side navigation, profile dashboard, metrics, progress, media rails, training levels, team hierarchy, and utility screens.
- Preserve the connected team tree and strengthen its raised nodes and connector hierarchy on both desktop and phones.
- Update shared lecture, series, level, empty-state, form, dialog, tab, and button styling so every route inherits the system consistently.

## Admin experience
- Reframe the admin workspace as a dense raised control surface with elevated statistics, inset tab navigation, structured forms, and consistent content panels.
- Preserve all existing management actions and information density.

## Motion and responsiveness
- Use subtle rise, hover elevation, and limited floating motion only where it reinforces depth.
- Preserve reduced-motion behavior.
- Reflow connected compositions into intentional vertical sequences on phones with no horizontal overflow or compressed text.

## Validation
- Verify the landing page and representative public, member, training, team, authentication, and admin screens.
- Check desktop and phone layouts for overflow, contrast, spacing, readable controls, and intact interactions.
- Confirm every content route has complete route-specific sharing metadata and the preview builds cleanly.
